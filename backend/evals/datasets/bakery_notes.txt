Call notes with Anita (owner, Sweet Crumbs Bakery, Nashik), 3 March. Prepared by Karan.

Anita wants an online ordering website where customers can pick a pickup slot for their cakes and bread.
Payments only through Razorpay. She was very clear about that and does not want any other gateway.
Maybe we add a loyalty stamp card later, but she is not sure yet and wants to see the first version.
Delivery is not needed. Pickup only, from the shop counter.
The site must go live before Diwali, the hard deadline is 20 October.
She mentioned her nephew might design the logo, but nothing is decided.
She wants an admin panel so her staff can mark orders as ready for pickup.
Stock sync with their existing Tally accounting: she will check with her accountant whether Tally exports work and let us know.
Small talk: it rained all week in Nashik, and her cat knocked over the display stand yesterday.
She did not mention a budget figure at all.
