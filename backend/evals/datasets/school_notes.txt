Meeting notes: Greenfield Public School, Pune. Attendees: Mr. Kulkarni (principal), Divya (project lead). 18 June.

The school needs a parent portal where parents can view their child's attendance and fee dues.
Fee payments must work through the school's existing PayU account. No other gateway will be integrated.
Teachers should be able to upload homework as PDF files; the principal said maybe images could be allowed in a later phase.
SMS alerts for absence were requested at first, but the principal later decided on email only because of cost.
Data must stay on servers located in India. This is a policy requirement from the trustees.
The principal is not sure whether a mobile app is needed or if a responsive website is enough.
The portal must be ready before the new academic session starts on 1 July.
No budget figure was shared; Mr. Kulkarni will check with the trustees.
Divya noted that the school cafeteria menu was discussed briefly, but it is out of scope.
